Description

Transform your wellness routine with the Maxxus MX-K306-01-ZF Cedar 3-Person Near Zero EMF FAR Infrared Sauna. Crafted from natural Canadian Red Cedar, this sauna has a distinct aroma and a near-zero EMF rating. Designed to comfortably accommodate up to three users, it features seven PureTech™ Near Zero EMF carbon heating panels that provide efficient and consistent heat. The sauna also includes oversized chromotherapy lighting to enhance your mood and promote healing. With its user-friendly LED control panel, built-in Bluetooth audio system, and easy assembly, this sauna brings the spa experience to your home

Key Features

  • Near Zero EMF Heating Panels: Utilizes seven PureTech™ carbon heating panels to deliver efficient and consistent heat with minimal electromagnetic field exposure.
  • Oversized Chromotherapy Lighting System: Includes an oversized chromotherapy lighting system, with a red light therapy feature starting in 2024 models, to enhance mood and promote healing.
  • Bluetooth Audio System: Comes with a built-in Bluetooth audio system, allowing you to enjoy music or guided sessions during your sauna experience.
  • Easy Assembly: Designed for easy, clasp-together assembly, making setup straightforward and hassle-free.
  • User-Friendly Control Panel: Features an interior LED control panel (updated to a touch screen control panel in 2024), providing intuitive operation.

Dimensions & Specifications

  • Exterior Dimensions: 64" (W) x 44" (D) x 75" (H) (roof overhang adds 5.9" to depth)
  • Interior Dimensions: 58" (W) x 39" (D) x 68" (H)
  • Wood Type: Natural Canadian Red Cedar
  • Heating Panels: 7 PureTech™ Near Zero EMF Carbon panels
  • Electrical Requirements: 120V/20amp dedicated receptacle (consult a certified electrician)
  • Control Panel: Interior LED control panel (updated to touch screen control panel in 2024)
  • Audio System: Bluetooth and MP3 Aux Connection with built-in speakers
  • Assembly: Clasp-together assembly
