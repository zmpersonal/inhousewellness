Of the 14 saunas with an 8 kW heater in the InHouse Wellness Verified Sauna Database, the manufacturers of 10 state a 240 V, 40 A circuit for the heater; for the other 4, the manufacturer sources we've verified describe no heater circuit.

Each row quotes the manufacturer's own words, with its grade, source and the date we verified it. InHouse Wellness does not calculate a breaker or wire size: those depend on your local code and your electrician.

11 of the 14 listed models are from Salus.
