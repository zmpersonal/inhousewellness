## The finding inside the batch: the archive is the worst of it

The three ARCHIVED products carry **15 of the 25 claim sentences**, and they are
a different order of severity from anything ACTIVE:

> *"…helps **eliminate heavy metals, toxins, and impurities** from the body."*
> *"…**boost collagen production** and promote youthful skin."*
> *"…help **relieve joint pain, arthritis symptoms**, and muscle soreness."*
> *"The sauna helps **burn calories, improve metabolism**, and enhance skin clarity."*

**"Burns calories" is named in rule 4's own banned list.** These are the oldest
supplier descriptions in the catalogue, and the newer ACTIVE ones are visibly
cleaner — the copy standard improved and **the archive preserved the old one**.

That is the argument for your ruling that archived is in scope. A restored
product brings 2023 marketing copy back to a 2026 storefront, and nothing on the
site would flag it.

## CUT AND SUBSTITUTE — 13

### The santorini pattern — 5 products publish an mG figure

`dynamic-santorini` · `dynamic-vila-grande` · `dynamic-cordoba-2-person` ·
`dynamic-3person-sauna` (ARCH) · `dynamic-6-person-sauna` (ARCH)

Each gets the measured figure plus the question, because the figure is the thing
a competitor will not print:

> Rated at **3 mG**. Ask any seller what distance that figure was measured at, including us.

**`dynamic-cordoba` also publishes the distance** — *"2–3 inches from the
heating panels"* — so it gets the stronger form:

> Rated at **3 mG at 2–3 inches from the panel**, which is the strict end of how this industry measures. Ask any seller for the distance as well as the number.

### Worked examples

**`dynamic-vila-grande-4-person-full-spectrum-sauna` · ACTIVE · $4,299**

> ~~Near infrared works at the skin's surface to **support cell renewal, reduce inflammation, and stimulate mitochondrial activity**.~~
> **→** Near infrared sits at the shortest wavelength of the three and is absorbed closest to the skin. Full spectrum means near and mid on top of far. The cabin is Hemlock, seats four, runs on **120V**, and the panels are rated at **3 MG** — ask any seller what distance that was measured at, including us.

**`dynamic-cordoba-2-person-full-spectrum-sauna` · ACTIVE · $3,299**

> ~~FAR infrared reaches the deepest tissue, **activating sweat glands and driving the detoxification response that comes from fat-based sweating**.~~
> **→** FAR infrared is the longest of the three wavelengths and the one that does most of the heating. Rated at **3 MG at 2–3 inches from the panel**, which is the strict end of how this industry measures.

**`dynamic-versailles-elite-2-person-infrared-sauna` · ACTIVE · $2,299**

> Featuring 6 low EMF FAR infrared carbon heating panels, this sauna delivers soothing, even heat ~~that supports detoxification, relieves muscle tension, reduces stress, and improves overall circulation~~.
> **→** Featuring **6 low EMF FAR infrared carbon heating panels** in a **2-person Canadian Hemlock** cabin, with Bluetooth and chromotherapy lighting fitted.
> *(also cut: "luxurious and **restorative** wellness experience" → "a 2-person cabin with the panels, the audio and the lighting all fitted as standard")*
>
> ⚠️ **My first draft of this substitution said "on a standard 120V outlet". This
> product does not publish a voltage.** The fact-verification pass caught it
> before it shipped — every other Dynamic product in the batch names 120V and I
> carried the assumption across. This is the substitution method failing in
> exactly the way it exists to prevent, and it is why the check runs on every
> proposed figure rather than on a sample.

**`1-2-person-infrared-sauna-model-number-low-emf-hemlock-bluetooth` · ACTIVE · $1,999**

> ~~Built for **stress relief, detoxification, and muscle recovery**, this sauna is your personal escape to better health and relaxation.~~
> **→** At **$1,999** this is the cheapest way into an infrared cabin we sell. Hemlock, seats one to two.

**`dymanic-lugano-elite-infrared-sauna` · ARCHIVED · $3,499 — the worst in the batch**
