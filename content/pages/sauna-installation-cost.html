We do not publish an installation cost. Not a figure, not a range, not a regional average, not a default. That is a decision, made in September 2026 and unchanged since, and it is worth saying plainly because every other page on this search result does publish one.

An average is not a quote. What electrical work costs you depends on your panel's spare capacity, the run from panel to cabin, your local code, whether a permit and inspection are required, and what electricians charge where you live this month. A single national number hides all of that and reads as authoritative precisely because it is specific.

What we can tell you exactly: the $1,800 tier is InHouse Wellness's delivery and assembly, it is all-in for that work, and the electrician is a separate, third-party bill that is never folded into it.
