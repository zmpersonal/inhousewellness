An 8 kW traditional sauna, three 45-minute sessions a week, uses 936 kWh a year. That is about $172 at the US average rate, $277 in Massachusetts, $123 in Nevada. The kilowatt-hours are fixed; your state's rate decides the dollars.

The formula is kW × hours × sessions per week × 52 × your state's rate, and the rate comes from the US Energy Information Administration's residential series for period 2026-06. Enter a ZIP below and the calculator resolves it to a state and uses that state's published rate. If your ZIP does not resolve — a PO box, a single building, a ZIP that straddles a state line, a US territory — you get a coverage gap that says which, and never a national average dressed up as your rate.

For 92 of our 139 priced saunas the manufacturer publishes no rated power at all. We read 142 manuals and 3,569 pages to establish that; 90 of them state what to wire and never what the unit draws.
