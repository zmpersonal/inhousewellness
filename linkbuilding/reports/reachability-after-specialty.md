## Reachable is not the same as accepted

**4 of the 8 name a profession he does not hold.** That is not an attribution problem — the byline stays correct either way — but the journalist set the requirement and the journalist decides. They are counted as reachable because he is eligible to answer and can be described accurately while doing it; they are flagged because a pitch may still be declined on fit.

The other 4 — living-donor transplant complications, head lice in Black hair, hyperpigmentation, and the psychological response to surviving a mass shooting — name no required profession at all.
