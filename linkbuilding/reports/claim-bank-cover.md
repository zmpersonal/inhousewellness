# Claim bank — review request

## For Timur Alptunaer, MD · prepared 2026-09-15

What follows is 30 claims with their hedges, their do-not-say lists and their
citations. It is long because it has to be exact. **You are being asked three
questions; everything else is supporting material.**

## 1. Does anything in the `strong` tier not belong there?

Eight claims are graded `strong` — the system's grade, not yours. A strong
claim is pitched with the most confidence and the least hedging, so a mis-grade
there costs more than one anywhere else. Sound but over-graded → **Approve with
edits**, saying what it should be. Not supportable at all → **Strike**.

## 2. Does anything in the `preliminary` tier need striking?

Seven claims rest on mechanistic, small or single-study evidence — nine
participants in one case, cellular markers rather than outcomes in others.
They are collected on the first page so you can **strike the entire tier in one
decision** if you would rather not have early-stage findings attributed to you.
That is a legitimate answer and costs little — the strong and moderate tiers
carry the topics that actually get asked about.

## 3. Are there claims where cohort size would change your answer?

**This is the one we cannot answer for you, and the reason matters.**

Sample size is missing for 35 of the 47 citations. It reads *sample size not
stated*, which means not stated — not inferred, not summed from subgroups, not
recalled from memory. The index returns abstracts, full text was unavailable for
every paper here, and only 12 citations state a figure verbatim in the abstract.

So **mark any claim where you would want the cohort size before deciding.** Those
get pulled and the numbers read out of the papers first. We would rather hold a
claim than have you approve one on an assumption about its size.

Journal names are absent for the same reason — dropped rather than derived from
the DOI prefix, since `10.1001` covers JAMA, JAMA Internal Medicine and JAMA
Cardiology alike. Every DOI resolves to the paper of record; use them where the
venue matters to your judgement.

## What approval commits you to

**Standing attribution of these specific sentences, with their hedges attached,
without you reviewing each individual pitch.** Not a topic area, not a general
endorsement of sauna or cold exposure. A claim not in this bank cannot be
pitched, and that is enforced in code rather than by anyone remembering. Nothing
has been sent and nothing can be until this is signed: the system records the
bank as `awaiting_review` and refuses to run any drafting step.

## Two things worth knowing before you start

**Every citation was retrieved from the live literature index, not written from
memory.** Titles are stored exactly as returned and re-checked automatically; if
a stored citation stops matching its identifier the bank fails closed.

**One claim records a null result on purpose.** `heat-vascular-limits-01` is a
randomised trial in coronary artery disease that found no benefit, sitting
alongside the positive observational findings. It is there because a source who
volunteers the trial that did not work is more credible than one who does not.

*Marking is per claim throughout: Approve ☐ · Approve with edits ☐ · Strike ☐.
The sign-off page is at the end.*
