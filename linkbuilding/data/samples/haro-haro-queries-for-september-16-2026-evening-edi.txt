Reporting a feature for Deel Works. Most benefits and eligibility architecture

you're not who I'm looking for. Not looking for: benefits brokers, consultants,

threshold in hours per week? When an employee reduced hours ahead of retirement,
what happened to their coverage and retirement contributions? Did you carve out

an hours reduction because of what it would do to eligibility? What did you tell
the employee? This is the one I most want to hear about. Who made that call —

retirement match, PTO accrual, life insurance, something else? Which causes the
most trouble in practice? Job structure and pay Has your company changed a job

the constraint, and who had to approve it? Have you had someone stay in a role
past the point where the job description still described what they did? What
happened? HSA and Medicare Has an employee kept contributing to an HSA after
becoming Medicare-eligible, or been caught by the six-month retroactive Part A
rule? What happened, who noticed, and what did it cost them to unwind? Was it
caught at enrollment, at payroll, or at tax filing? How far downstream matters
to me. What do you send employees approaching 65, and at what age? If it's
nothing beyond the summary plan description, please say so — that's a finding,
not an embarrassment. Elder care Have you proposed an elder care benefit —
referral, subsidy, backup care? What was the response and the reasoning? If
turned down, what was the specific objection?
